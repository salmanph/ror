Ramadan Kareem

May this blessed month bring peace, mercy, and barakah to you and your loved ones.

Du‘a

اللَّهُمَّ بَلِّغْنَا رَمَضَانَ وَأَعِنَّا عَلَى صِيَامِهِ وَقِيَامِهِ وَتَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا
O Allah, let us reach Ramadan, help us to fast and pray, and accept it from us. Ameen.

Message of Blessings

May your days be filled with sincerity, your nights with remembrance, and your home with light. May Allah accept your fasting, prayers, and good deeds, and grant your family peace and guidance.

رمضان كريم 🌙
Greetings from HON. SALMAN ALI
May Allah accept your good deeds • Ameen
